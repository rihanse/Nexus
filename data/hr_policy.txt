HR Policy Document

Employees are entitled to 12 casual leaves per year.
Employees are entitled to 10 sick leaves per year.
The notice period is 60 days.
Work from home is allowed for 2 days per month with manager approval.
Maternity leave is available as per company policy and applicable government regulations.
Employees should inform their manager in advance for planned leave.
Emergency leave should be informed within 24 hours.
Leave requests require manager approval.
