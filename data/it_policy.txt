IT Policy Document

Employees can raise IT tickets for laptop issues, VPN problems, Outlook or email issues, printer issues, network access, and software installation.
Before creating a ticket, the system should check known outages, planned maintenance, and duplicate open tickets.
IT tickets can be assigned only by IT Team or Admin.
Only IT Team or Admin can resolve tickets.
Employees can view only their own tickets.
Asset requests include laptop, monitor, keyboard, mouse, VPN token, and software license.
Asset approval flow is Employee Request, Manager Approval, IT Approval, Inventory Validation, and Fulfillment.
