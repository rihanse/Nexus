FINANCE & REIMBURSEMENT POLICY
Company: Enterprise Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Department: Finance
Version: 4.0 | Effective Date: April 1, 2024 (FY 2024-25)

─────────────────────────────────────
1. EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT LIMITS
─────────────────────────────────────
Travel: Local conveyance up to INR 5,000 per day. Air travel requires pre-approval.
Hotel: INR 4,000/night (Tier-1 cities), INR 2,500/night (Tier-2/3 cities).
Internet Allowance: INR 1,000 per month for permanent WFH employees (receipt required).
Food & Meals: INR 500 per person per day for team/client meetings.
Client Entertainment: INR 5,000 per meeting with prior manager approval.

─────────────────────────────────────
2. REIMBURSEMENT SUBMISSION GUIDELINES
─────────────────────────────────────
All claims must be submitted within 30 calendar days of the expense date.
A valid receipt is mandatory for all expenses above INR 200.
Expenses must be submitted through the Enterprise Finance Copilot.
Approved reimbursements are paid in the next payroll cycle.

─────────────────────────────────────
3. INCOME TAX — NEW TAX REGIME (FY 2024-25)
─────────────────────────────────────
Slabs:
  - Up to INR 3,00,000: Nil
  - INR 3,00,001 to 6,00,000: 5%
  - INR 6,00,001 to 9,00,000: 10%
  - INR 9,00,001 to 12,00,000: 15%
  - INR 12,00,001 to 15,00,000: 20%
  - Above INR 15,00,000: 30%
Standard Deduction: INR 75,000 (New Regime) / INR 50,000 (Old Regime).
Employees must declare their tax regime choice by April 30th of the financial year.
Investment declarations under the Old Regime must be submitted by January 31st.

─────────────────────────────────────
4. PROVIDENT FUND (PF) CONTRIBUTION
─────────────────────────────────────
Employee PF contribution: 12% of basic salary per month.
Employer PF contribution: 12% of basic salary per month.
PF is deducted monthly and reflected in the payslip.
Employees can check PF balance via the EPFO portal using their UAN number.

─────────────────────────────────────
5. SALARY DISBURSEMENT
─────────────────────────────────────
Salaries are disbursed on the last working day of every month.
Payslips are available in the HR portal by the 1st of the following month.
Discrepancies must be reported to finance@company.com within 7 days.

─────────────────────────────────────
6. INVESTMENT DECLARATION DEADLINE
─────────────────────────────────────
Employees opting for the Old Tax Regime must submit Form 12BB by January 31st.
Proof of investments must be submitted to Finance by February 15th.
Failure to submit proof will result in higher TDS computation without exemptions.

For finance queries, contact finance@company.com.
