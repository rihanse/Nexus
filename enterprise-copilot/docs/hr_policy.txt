HUMAN RESOURCES POLICY DOCUMENT
Company: Enterprise Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Version: 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

─────────────────────────────────────
1. NOTICE PERIOD POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────
All employees are required to serve a mandatory notice period upon resignation or termination.
- Regular Employees: 30 calendar days notice is required.
- Managers and Senior Staff: 60 calendar days notice is required.
The company may waive the notice period by paying equivalent salary in lieu of notice.

─────────────────────────────────────
2. LEAVE POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────

2.1 Casual Leave (CL)
Employees are entitled to 10 days of casual leave per calendar year.
Maximum 3 consecutive casual leave days may be taken at a stretch.
Casual leave cannot be carried forward and lapses if unused.

2.2 Sick Leave (SL)
Employees are entitled to 7 days of sick leave per calendar year.
A medical certificate is mandatory for sick leave exceeding 2 consecutive days.

2.3 Annual Leave (AL) / Privilege Leave (PL)
Employees are entitled to 15 days of annual leave per calendar year.
Annual leave must be applied at least 7 calendar days in advance.
Unused annual leave can be accumulated up to a maximum of 30 days.
Up to 10 unused annual leave days may be encashed once per calendar year (January window).

2.4 Maternity Leave
Female employees with at least 80 days of service are entitled to 26 weeks (182 days) of fully paid maternity leave.

2.5 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 5 working days of paid paternity leave within 30 days of the birth or adoption of a child.

─────────────────────────────────────
3. WORK FROM HOME (WFH) POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────
Employees are permitted to work from home for up to 2 days per week.
WFH days must be pre-approved by the direct reporting manager.
Employees on WFH must remain reachable during working hours (9:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST).

─────────────────────────────────────
4. LEAVE ENCASHMENT
─────────────────────────────────────
Employees may encash up to 10 unused annual leave days per year.
Applications must be submitted to HR by January 15th of each year.
Encashment is calculated at the basic salary rate per day and is taxable.

─────────────────────────────────────
5. GENERAL PROVISIONS
─────────────────────────────────────
All leave requests must be submitted through the Enterprise HR Copilot system.
The company reserves the right to amend this policy with 30 days advance notice.
For queries, contact HR at hr@company.com.
