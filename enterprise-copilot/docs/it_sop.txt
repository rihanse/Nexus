IT STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
Company: Enterprise Solutions Pvt. Ltd.
Department: Information Technology
Version: 2.1 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

─────────────────────────────────────
1. LAPTOP / HARDWARE REQUEST PROCESS
─────────────────────────────────────
Employees requiring a new or replacement laptop must submit an Asset Request through the IT Copilot system.
- Step 1: Employee submits request with asset type, justification, and business need.
- Step 2: Direct manager reviews and approves/rejects within 2 working days.
- Step 3: Upon manager approval, request is forwarded to the IT team.
- Step 4: IT team procures and configures the asset within 5–7 working days.
- Step 5: Asset is handed over with a signed receipt. All assets remain company property.
Replacement requests for lost or damaged equipment must be accompanied by a written explanation. 
Deliberate damage may result in cost recovery from the employee.

─────────────────────────────────────
2. VPN SETUP AND TROUBLESHOOTING
─────────────────────────────────────
The company uses GlobalProtect VPN for all remote access.
Setup: Download the GlobalProtect client from the IT portal. Use your company email and AD credentials.
MFA is mandatory — enroll in Microsoft Authenticator before first use.
Common Troubleshooting Steps:
- Cannot connect: Ensure internet is active. Restart the GlobalProtect app.
- Frequent disconnections: Check firewall/antivirus settings; whitelist GlobalProtect.
- Slow VPN speeds: Connect to the nearest regional gateway (IT portal lists all gateways).
- Certificate errors: Contact IT helpdesk — certificate renewal required.
If the issue persists after troubleshooting, raise a ticket with issue_type = "vpn" and priority = "high".

─────────────────────────────────────
3. SOFTWARE INSTALLATION POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────
Only software listed on the Approved Software List (ASL) may be installed on company devices.
Approved categories: Development IDEs (VS Code, IntelliJ, PyCharm), Communication (Teams, Slack, Zoom),
Productivity (MS Office, Notion), Browsers (Chrome, Firefox, Edge), Security (Crowdstrike, BitLocker).
To request new software: Submit a ticket with issue_type = "software" including the software name, 
vendor, business justification, and license cost. Unauthorized software installation is a policy violation 
and may result in disciplinary action. IT reserves the right to remotely uninstall unauthorized software.

─────────────────────────────────────
4. TICKET PRIORITY DEFINITIONS & SLAs
─────────────────────────────────────
Low Priority: Minor inconveniences with no impact on productivity (e.g., printer not working).
  SLA: First response within 8 business hours. Resolution within 5 business days.

Medium Priority: Issues impacting individual productivity (e.g., software crash, email issues).
  SLA: First response within 4 business hours. Resolution within 2 business days.

High Priority: Issues significantly impacting work output (e.g., VPN failure, laptop malfunction).
  SLA: First response within 1 business hour. Resolution within 4 business hours.

Critical Priority: Complete work stoppage affecting multiple users or business-critical systems.
  SLA: Immediate response (15 minutes). Resolution within 1 business hour.
  Escalation: CTO and IT Head are auto-notified for all Critical tickets.

─────────────────────────────────────
5. PASSWORD POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────
All company system passwords must meet the following criteria:
- Minimum 12 characters in length.
- Must include: uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Passwords must be reset every 90 days. Reminders are sent 14 days before expiry.
- Previous 10 passwords cannot be reused.
- Account lockout occurs after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. Contact IT to unlock.

─────────────────────────────────────
6. DATA BACKUP POLICY
─────────────────────────────────────
All work-related data must be stored on company-approved cloud storage (OneDrive / SharePoint).
Local-only storage is not permitted for critical business data.
Automated backups run daily at 2:00 AM IST for all cloud-synced data.
Employees are responsible for ensuring their work is saved to the cloud before end of each working day.
Data recovery requests must be submitted to IT within 30 days of data loss.

For IT support, raise a ticket via the copilot or email it@company.com.
